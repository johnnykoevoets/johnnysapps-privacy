Privacy Policy — Score The Party

Effective date: 19 October 2026

Our privacy approach

Score The Party is a shared party game. Event hosts and players may contribute names, scores, notes and optional evidence photos. Shared events use Google Firebase to make that information available to authorized event participants. Please avoid uploading photos or information about others without their permission.

1. Who operates the app

Score The Party is provided by Johnny's Apps. This policy explains how personal information is processed through the Android app and its multiplayer services.

2. Information processed

Depending on how you use the app, information may include event names and settings; participant names or nicknames; game captures, categories, descriptions, scores and timestamps; optional evidence photos; event membership and host/guest roles; edit and deletion requests; technical user identifiers; and notification registration tokens. A photo may reveal the appearance of people shown in it.

The app uses Firebase Anonymous Authentication, which assigns a technical user identifier without requiring users to provide an email address or password to join through that mechanism.

3. Photos and camera access

Users may choose photos from their gallery or capture a photo for game evidence. Selected images are processed into JPEG image data and can be stored as part of shared capture records in Google Cloud Firestore. They are not merely stored on the uploading device. Event participants with access to the shared event may see associated evidence photos. Camera access is used for user-initiated photo capture; access to selected gallery media is used for user-initiated photo selection.

Do not upload photos of other people without an appropriate basis or permission. Avoid sensitive, intimate, unlawful or otherwise inappropriate material.

4. Cloud services and sharing

The app uses Google Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Firebase Cloud Messaging. Shared event information and captures are synchronized through Firestore. Hosts and joined players may access event information according to their roles and the app's access controls. Firebase processes technical information necessary to provide these services. Google may process service data under its applicable terms and privacy documentation: Firebase Privacy and Security.

Other event participants may see the names, scores, descriptions and evidence you contribute. A host can remove a participant's access, but this does not automatically erase captures previously contributed by that participant.

5. Notifications

The app uses Firebase Cloud Messaging to support event-related notifications. This may involve a device registration token and event membership information. The app may also schedule reminders locally on the device. You can manage notification permissions in Android settings.

6. Local data, downloads and exports

The app also maintains local event and gameplay data on the device. Hosts may export event recaps or videos. Once a user saves or shares an exported file outside the app, that copy is controlled by the user and the destination service, and deleting an event in the app will not delete separately saved copies.

7. Retention and deletion

Shared event information is not automatically deleted merely because an event ends. A host can delete a shared event, which initiates deletion of the event and its associated captures, member records, edit requests and invitation mapping from Firestore. A host can also delete captures individually; guest edit or deletion requests may require host approval. A participant can leave an event or be removed by its host, but their existing contributions may remain in the shared event.

Deletion from active app records does not necessarily remove information from independently exported files, other participants' screenshots or copies, or provider backups immediately. Local app information may remain on devices until deleted through the app, app storage is cleared or the app is uninstalled, subject to device backup behavior. We do not promise a fixed automatic deletion schedule.

To request access, correction or deletion of personal information, email johnnysapps@outlook.com and identify the relevant event and information. We may need additional details to locate the records and verify that the request is authorized. Requests will be handled in accordance with applicable law.

8. Purpose and legal basis

We process information to operate shared events, maintain scores and evidence, manage access, send requested gameplay notifications, provide support and maintain service security. Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, processing may rely on performance of the service requested by users, legitimate interests in operating and securing the service, or consent where required. People uploading information about others are responsible for ensuring that sharing is appropriate and lawful.

9. Security and international processing

Shared data is processed using Firebase infrastructure and app-level access controls. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Firebase service infrastructure may process data outside the user's country; relevant provider arrangements and applicable safeguards may apply.

10. Children

Score The Party is a social party game and is not designed specifically for children. Users should not upload children's personal information or photographs without appropriate permission and a lawful basis. Any age eligibility or supervision requirements presented in the app or store listing should be followed.

11. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or portability of personal information, and to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. Contact us using the address above. We may be unable to identify a particular anonymous user or locate an event without enough information from the requester.

12. Changes

We may update this policy as the app, its service providers or legal requirements change. Material changes will be reflected by an updated effective date.
