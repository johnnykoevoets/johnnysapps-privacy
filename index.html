Privacy Policy — PulseLead

Our privacy approach

We believe leadership observations deserve responsible handling. PulseLead Individual is designed to keep your information on your own device. Future Business and Enterprise editions are expected to introduce secure organizational collaboration and cloud storage.

1. Scope

This policy describes the privacy approach of Johnny's Apps for PulseLead Individual and the planned PulseLead Business and Enterprise editions.

The Individual edition is the currently relevant product. Business and Enterprise functionality described below is planned and is not a statement that those services are already available.

2. PulseLead Individual

Information you enter

Users may record organizational structures, teams, names, behavioral observations, supporting notes, and related leadership information.

Local storage

PulseLead Individual is designed to store user-entered information locally on the user's device. Johnny's Apps does not operate a cloud database for Individual user observations and does not receive those observations through the app.

Backups and exports

Users may manually export or restore their information. Exported files are controlled by the user. If users choose to transfer those files to email, cloud storage, or another service, that service's privacy practices may apply.

Reminders

PulseLead Individual may provide reminders to encourage consistent observation and reflection. These reminders are intended to operate on the user's device.

Deletion

Users can remove information using available app functionality. Uninstalling the app generally removes its locally stored app data, subject to device operating system behavior and any backups or exports retained separately by the user.

3. PulseLead Business and Enterprise — Planned

Future Business and Enterprise editions are expected to support collaboration between authorized users, including shared observations and organizational reporting.

These editions are currently planned to use Google Firebase or related cloud services for data storage, authentication, and synchronization.

Depending on the final implementation, information processed may include user account details, organizational relationships, observations, notes, and access permissions.

Before launching these editions, Johnny's Apps will update this policy to describe the actual processing activities, service providers, retention periods, security arrangements, user rights, and responsibilities of participating organizations.

4. Personal and Employee Information

PulseLead may be used to record information about identifiable individuals. Users should enter only information that is relevant, accurate, appropriate, and lawful for their intended purpose.

Users and participating organizations are responsible for complying with applicable employment, privacy, and data protection requirements when recording observations about other people.

5. Data Security

PulseLead Individual relies on local device storage. Users should protect their devices with appropriate access controls and handle exported files securely.

Security arrangements for Business and Enterprise will be documented when those editions are implemented.

6. Children

PulseLead is intended for professional, educational, coaching, and organizational use. It is not designed as a consumer service directed at children.

Any use involving information about minors must comply with applicable laws and organizational policies.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, individuals may have rights relating to access, correction, deletion, and other processing of their personal information.

For Individual, information is stored locally and Johnny's Apps generally cannot access or retrieve user-entered observations. Requests concerning information recorded by an organization should ordinarily be directed to that organization.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when functionality changes, new editions become available, or legal requirements change. The effective date will be updated accordingly.
